## 3. Division 296 — the tax on large balances

Used on: **07 Super Cheatsheet**, **08 Structures**

| Claim | Stated as | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Status | Passed both Houses 10 March 2026 | Ulton (11 March 2026) |
| Start | 1 July 2026, applying to 2026–27 earnings | Ulton; William Buck; SMSF Adviser |
| $3m tier | Additional tax on earnings attributable to the balance above $3m, taking the effective rate on that portion to **around 30%** | Multiple |
| $10m tier | A further tier, effective rate on that portion **around 40%** | William Buck; Ulton |
| Unrealised gains | Excluded — applies to realised earnings | Both |
| Indexation | $3m in $150,000 steps; $10m in $500,000 steps | Both |
| First measurement | Balances at 30 June 2027 | SuperGuide; Ulton |

> **Deliberate imprecision at the top tier.** Sources describe the above-$10m tier differently —
> one as "an additional 25%", another as "an additional 10%" on top of the first tier — while
> agreeing the **effective** rate lands near 40%. Sheet 07 therefore states effective rates
> ("around 30%", "around 40%") rather than marginal add-ons, and carries the line: *"This is new
> law with its first measurement date still ahead… confirm the current position before acting on
> it."* The $10m tier is in any case well outside the audience.

## 4. Personal income tax

Used on: **04 Next Dollar**, **06 Bonus Playbook**, **12 Freedom Guide**

| Bracket (resident, 2026–27) | Rate | With 2% Medicare levy |
|---|---:|---:|
| $0 – $18,200 | Nil | — |
| $18,201 – $45,000 | 15% | — |
| $45,001 – $135,000 | 30% | 32% |
| $135,001 – $190,000 | 37% | 39% |
| $190,001 + | 45% | 47% |

Source: multiple AU tax summaries for 2026–27, which note the first bracket fell from 16% to 15%
as the second step of the cuts legislated in 2025. Sheets 04 and 06 show only the three brackets
above $45,000, which is the whole of the audience.

Derived and shown on sheet 04: the gap between the with-levy marginal rate and the 15% rate inside
super — 17, 24 and 32 percentage points respectively; and 17 points where Division 293 applies
(47% against 30%).

## 7. Structural and legal statements (no figures)

Used on: **08 Structures**, **10 Estate**

These are stated as general positions, each hedged with "generally" or "typically", and each
carries a disclaimer directing the reader to a qualified adviser, accountant or estate lawyer:

- super is held in trust and is not automatically an estate asset;
- property held as joint tenants typically passes by survivorship, outside the will; tenants in
  common passes through the estate;
- the four nomination types and whether each binds the trustee; binding lapsing nominations
  typically expire after three years;
- who may validly be nominated — spouse, child, financial dependant, interdependency relationship,
  or legal personal representative;
- how income is taxed in each of the six structures, and the 50% CGT discount being available to
  individuals and trusts but not companies.

## 8b. The two newest sheets

**11 Conversation Swipe File** asserts no figures at all. Every one of its seven templates is a
question to ask someone else, and the page says so: *"These are templates for asking questions, not
advice about what to do with the answers."* The only substantive claim is procedural — that a salary
sacrifice arrangement must be in place before the income is earned — which is the standard position
and is stated as a reason the template exists, not as tax advice.

**12 Freedom Guide** introduces no new figures. Everything in it is already registered above: the
47% top marginal rate with the Medicare levy (§4), preservation age 60 from 1 July 2024 (§2), and
the 2021 change to income protection terms (§5). The three stat blocks in the book reuse those three
numbers and nothing else.

**07's eight shortcuts** restate figures from §1 and §3 — the $32,500 cap, the $500,000 carry-forward
threshold, the $250,000 Division 293 threshold and the $1,935,000 CGT cap — and add one procedural
point: binding lapsing nominations typically expire after three years, which is registered in §7.
Each shortcut carries an explicit trade-off line, so no shortcut reads as a recommendation.

## 9. Deliberately not claimed

Marketing claims that appear in Prospa's published material were **left out** of every sheet,
because the database's own source register flags them as needing compliance review before reuse:

- "500+ Satisfied Customers"
- "50+ Years of Experience"
- any award or achievement name
- any testimonial
- any statement about returns, outcomes or performance
- any named financial product, fund, insurer or platform

No sheet contains a statistic about Prospa, a client outcome, or a market claim. The only
performance-adjacent numbers anywhere are **user-entered assumptions** in the calculators, each
shown with a limitations block stating that returns are not guaranteed.
