The Gap Years Map

The most common blind spot among high earners is not how much they have. It is when they can reach it. These are the years between the age you would like work to become optional and the age superannuation unlocks — and nothing inside super can fund them.

The rule that creates the gap. From 1 July 2024, preservation age is 60 for everyone who had not already reached it. If you want work to become optional before 60, every one of those years is funded from capital held outside superannuation.

The three ways this gap closes

There are only so many levers. Most plans use some of each.

1

Build outside super

Direct more of the surplus into capital you can reach before 60. The trade-off is giving up some of super's concessional treatment on the way in.

2

Move the date

Every year later shortens the bridge and lengthens the accumulation. It is the bluntest lever, and usually the one people reach for last.

3

Change the shape of the years

Part-time work, consulting or a staged exit reduces the net annual need. A smaller need across fewer years is a far smaller number.

Take this to a conversation

The bridge figure above is the question, not the answer. Which lever fits depends on your marginal tax rate, your existing structures, your debt and what you want those years to look like — and that is a conversation, not a calculator.

Important limitations. A simplified educational illustration. The bridge figure is an undiscounted sum in today's dollars and does not account for inflation, tax, returns earned during the drawdown, sequencing risk, the age pension, or any condition of release that may apply to you. Preservation rules, access conditions and the treatment of each asset type depend on your circumstances. General information only.
