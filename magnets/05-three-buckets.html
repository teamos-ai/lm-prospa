The shape of it

Not how much, but where. A balance sheet can be strong and still point almost entirely at one timeframe.

How much can you reach before 60?

This is the same money, cut a second way: everything in Today and Freedom against everything you have set aside for 60 and beyond.

The gap to look for

Someone can hold significant wealth in the family home and in super, and very little they can actually reach in between. Nothing is wrong with either asset. They are simply pointed at the same timeframe.

This worksheet exists to make that visible. It does not prescribe what should fill the gap, or whether the gap matters to you.
