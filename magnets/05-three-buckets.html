The shape of your balance sheet

Not how much, but where. A strong balance sheet can still be poorly aligned to what you want it to do.

The gap to look for

Someone may have significant wealth in their home and their super, and very little accessible capital to fund an earlier exit. Nothing is wrong with those assets — they are simply all pointed at the same timeframe. The purpose of the three-bucket view is to expose timing and access gaps, not to prescribe a particular investment.
