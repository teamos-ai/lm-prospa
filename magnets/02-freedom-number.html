Your Freedom Score

Four questions, about two minutes. At the end you get a score out of 100, the capital your chosen lifestyle actually calls for, and the age your current trajectory reaches it — which, for most people, is not the age they had in mind.

StaysPrivate

Calculated in your browser. Nothing is sent anywhere, by us or to us.

The one idea behind it

A portfolio supports a lifestyle, not a lump sum. Decide the annual income you want, divide by the rate you would draw it at, and you have a capital target. Everything else on the result page is the distance between that target and where you are headed.

          Important limitations. A simplified educational illustration, not a retirement forecast and not personal advice. It ignores tax, fees, investment volatility, the order in which returns arrive, changes in spending, superannuation, the age pension, other assets and much else. The withdrawal rate is a planning assumption, not a recommendation or a guarantee, and returns are not guaranteed — a real portfolio does not deliver the same number every year, and the sequence matters as much as the average. Your freedom age and the required annual figure are arithmetic on the assumptions you chose, so they move when those assumptions move. Moneysmart's retirement guidance notes that what you need varies with the lifestyle you want, and that planning should consider when super can be accessed and how any income gap is funded — moneysmart.gov.au.
