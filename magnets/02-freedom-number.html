Important limitations. A simplified educational illustration, not a retirement forecast and not personal advice. Every figure is shown in today's dollars, projected at a real return — your assumed return less inflation. It deliberately excludes tax, capital gains tax, franking credits, contribution tax, fees, property, the Age Pension, market volatility and the order in which returns arrive, debt, and estate considerations. Real portfolios do not deliver the same return every year, and sequence matters as much as average. Superannuation preservation age is 60 for everyone reaching it from 1 July 2024; the access age here is an input and should match your own circumstances. Moneysmart's retirement guidance notes that what you need varies with the lifestyle you want, and that planning should consider when super can be accessed and how any income gap is funded — moneysmart.gov.au.
