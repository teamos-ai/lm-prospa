The hard part

Nobody gets stuck on the answer. They get stuck on the first sentence.

Every other sheet in this pack ends in something you cannot do on your own — a figure only your accountant holds, a form only payroll can send, a nomination only your fund can confirm. None of it is complicated. It is simply unstarted, because the opening line was never written.

So here it is written, seven times over. Each one asks for something the person receiving it can read off a record already in front of them.

Every message on this sheet asks a closed question — one whose answer is a figure, a yes or no, or a document. Keep the ask narrow and the reply comes fast. Widen it and you have asked for advice, which is a different conversation.

General information only. These are templates for asking questions, not advice about what to do with the answers. Nothing here recommends a product, strategy or structure, and none of it considers your objectives, financial situation or needs. Whether any action is appropriate for you depends on your circumstances — seek personal advice before acting on anything you learn.
